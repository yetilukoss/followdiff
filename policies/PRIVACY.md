# FollowDiff Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

## The short version

FollowDiff does not collect, transmit, share, sell, or analyze your data. Everything it stores lives on your own device and nowhere else.

## What FollowDiff does

FollowDiff is a read-only browser extension that shows you who does not follow you back on Instagram, who your fans are, and who your mutuals are. It reads your follower and following lists from instagram.com using your own logged-in session and keeps snapshots locally so it can diff them over time.

## Data stored on your device

All snapshots are stored in chrome.storage.local on your machine. Snapshot data includes usernames, profile picture URLs, verified status, and follower/following counts. Nothing else is stored. A "keep list" of accounts you mark as important is also stored locally. You can delete all of it at any time with the "Clear all" button in the footer.

## Cookies

FollowDiff does not set, read, or use cookies of its own. It reads instagram.com cookies from your browser to call Instagram's API endpoints on your behalf, which is how it accesses your data. Those cookie values are never transmitted to us, never stored by the extension, and never used for anything except the requests you trigger.

## Network activity

FollowDiff talks to instagram.com only, plus image CDN hosts (fbcdn, cdninstagram) to display profile pictures. It does not talk to any other server. There is no backend, no account system, no analytics service, and no telemetry.

## What we do not do

We do not collect personal information. We do not read messages, comments, DMs, or private content. We do not sell or share data with third parties. We do not use advertising or tracking of any kind. We do not run remote code.

## Permissions, explained

- storage / unlimitedStorage: to save your snapshots on your device.
- sidePanel: to show FollowDiff in your browser's side panel.
- tabs: to open Instagram profile pages in a new tab when you click a person.
- host permissions (instagram.com, fbcdn, cdninstagram): to read your follower and following lists and your profile pictures.

## Children

FollowDiff is not directed at children under 13. It requires an Instagram account, which itself has minimum age requirements.
